A data structure decides how your data is arranged; an algorithm decides what you
do with it. This week covers the handful of each that every other course, book,
and interview assumes you know.

<Callout title="Before you start">
You need [Classes](/docs/week3/classes) — the structures here are built as classes —
and [Lists](/docs/week2/lists).
</Callout>

## What you'll learn

- What stacks, queues, and linked lists are, and when each one fits
- Five sorting algorithms, how each works step by step, and what they cost
- When to stop writing your own and use Python's built-ins
